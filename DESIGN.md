## Observed page structure

1. A compact navigation and an introductory hero with a large product statement, short supporting text, desktop/web calls to action, open-source link, and platform/subscription details.
2. A repeated list of product capabilities, indicating a potential continuously scrolling feature ticker. Whether it actually animates was not verified.
3. A numbered crafting-features section with an introductory headline, seven separately labeled capabilities, and a final application launch card. The text presents each capability with a title and concise explanation; media placement is unknown.
4. A numbered ownership section with an introductory statement and three benefits: open source, lasting ownership, and no middleman.
5. A numbered proof section containing three community film items with play controls.
6. A final conversion section with a short headline and two calls to action, followed by a detailed footer.
